# Spanish (`es`)

This file is read by anyone who uses Infinitary in Spanish, in Latin America, Spain or anywhere else, most of whom have never opened a terminal.
The register is calm, plain and warm neutral international Spanish, the way a careful colleague talks, with no regionalisms, never cute and never salesy.
The person is addressed as "tú" ("tu proyecto", "elige", "puedes"), never "usted" and never "vos".
Where grammar forces a gender for the person, the masculine default is used ("capitán", "despierto"), and a neutral form is preferred where one reads naturally ("Te damos la bienvenida" rather than "Bienvenido").
Questions open with ¿ and close with ?, quotation marks are « », a decimal comma is used ("1,6 GB"), and a space sits between a number and its unit or "%" ("3 min", "1,6 GB", "40 %").
The plural forms are "one", "many" and "other"; "many" takes "de" before a noun ("1.000.000 de líneas"), as Spanish reads a round million, and repeats the "other" wording before an adjective ("1.000.000 nuevas").
A count in a message without a plural form sits after a colon ("nuevos: 3", "eliminadas: 2") so the words agree with any number.
