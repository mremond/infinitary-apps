# German (`de`)

This file is read by anyone in Germany, Austria or Switzerland who uses Infinitary, most of whom have never opened a terminal.
The register is calm, plain and warm German, the way a careful colleague talks, never cute and never salesy.
The person is addressed as "du", lowercase, with "dein/deine" for what belongs to them, and imperatives use the short form ("Schreib", "Drück", "Wähl").
Where grammar forces a gender, the masculine default is used ("Kapitän", "der erste Offizier", "der Verfasser"), as German software usually does.
Quotation marks are German low-high quotes „ “, a dash between clauses is a spaced en dash " – ", a decimal comma is used ("1,6 GB"), and a space sits between a number and its unit or the percent sign ("3 Min.", "1,6 GB", "40 %").
The plural forms are "one" and "other"; menu items that open a dialog end in a spaced ellipsis ("Einstellungen …"), as macOS German does.
